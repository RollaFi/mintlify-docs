## Overview

Send up to 500 NGN bank payouts in one request. Nothing leaves your wallet until the business owner approves the batch.

<Steps>
  <Step title="You create the batch">
    Send the payouts to this endpoint. You get back a batch `reference` with status `checking`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We check every payout">
    In the background we work out each payout's fee, apply your limits and look up the account name at the bank. Payouts that fail a check are left out of the batch, with the reason. Nothing is debited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The owner approves">
    The status becomes `awaiting_approval` and the business owner gets an email. They log in to the dashboard and approve the batch with their authenticator app. They have **30 minutes** after the checks finish. After that the batch expires and you create it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The payouts are sent">
    Your wallet is debited and each payout is sent. Each one sends you a `payout.completed` or `payout.failed` [webhook](/api-reference/webhooks), with your `reference` as `merchant_reference`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Track the batch with [Get Bulk Payout](/api-reference/get-bulk-payout). Look up a single payout with [Verify Payout](/api-reference/verify-payout), using its `reference`.

<Note>
**Before you start**
- Bulk payouts are switched on per business. Contact support to have them switched on.
- NGN bank transfers only for now, and live keys only.
- One batch per wallet can be checking or awaiting approval at a time.
- Up to 500 payouts are sent per hour across all of a business's batches.
</Note>

## Authentication

| Header | Type | Required | Description |
|--------|------|----------|-------------|
| `Authorization` | string | Yes | Your secret key (`sk_live_xxx`) |
| `Content-Type` | string | Yes | `application/json` |

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="currency" type="string" required>
  The wallet to pay from. `NGN` only for now.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payouts" type="array" required>
  1 to 500 payouts.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ParamField body="reference" type="string" required>
      Your unique reference for this payout, up to 100 characters. It must not be used by any of your other payouts. You will see it as `merchant_reference` on webhooks and on Verify Payout.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
      The amount in naira, at most 2 decimal places (e.g. `5000` means ₦5,000).
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="bank_code" type="string" required>
      The recipient's bank code from [List Banks](/api-reference/banks).
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="account_number" type="string" required>
      The recipient's 10-digit account number.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="account_name" type="string">
      The name you expect on the account. If you send it and the bank's name does not match, that payout is left out of the batch. You do not need a name enquiry first: we look up every account ourselves.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="narration" type="string">
      A description for the transfer, up to 200 characters.
    </ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="fee_source" type="string" default="balance">
  Who pays each payout's fee. `balance`: the fee is added on top, so the recipient gets the full `amount`. `amount`: the fee is taken out of `amount`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="name" type="string">
  A label for the batch, up to 255 characters, shown on the dashboard. For example `November salaries`.
</ParamField>

## Response

The batch is created and the checks have started. Nothing has been debited.

<ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">
  Indicates if the request was successful.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="message" type="string">
  A summary, or the reason the request failed.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">
      Batch ID.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reference" type="string">
      Batch reference. Use it with [Get Bulk Payout](/api-reference/get-bulk-payout).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">
      Currency code.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">
      Always `checking` here.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_payouts" type="number">
      Number of payouts received.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fee_source" type="string">
      `balance` or `amount`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string">
      When the batch was created (ISO 8601).
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
