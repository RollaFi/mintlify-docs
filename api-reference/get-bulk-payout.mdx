## Overview

Returns a bulk payout's status, its totals, and every payout in it: whether it passed the checks and, once sent, how it is doing.

## Authentication

| Header | Type | Required | Description |
|--------|------|----------|-------------|
| `Authorization` | string | Yes | Your secret key (`sk_live_xxx`) |

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="reference" type="string" required>
  The batch `reference` (e.g. `BATCH-1795001234567-K3M9QX2A`) or `id` returned by [Bulk Payout](/api-reference/bulk-payout).
</ParamField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X GET "https://api.rollapay.com/payout/bulk/BATCH-1795001234567-K3M9QX2A" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_your_secret_key"
```

## Batch Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `checking` | We are checking each payout. Nothing is debited. |
| `awaiting_approval` | Checked. Waiting for the business owner to approve on the dashboard, until `approve_by`. |
| `processing` | Approved. Your wallet is debited and the payouts are with the banks. |
| `completed` | Every payout completed. |
| `completed_with_failures` | Every payout finished and at least one failed. Failed payouts are refunded to your wallet. |
| `cancelled` | Cancelled on the dashboard, or after three wrong authenticator codes. Nothing was sent. |
| `expired` | Not approved within 30 minutes of the checks. Nothing was sent; create the batch again. |

## Response

<ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">
  Indicates if the request was successful.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="message" type="string">
  `Bulk payout retrieved`, or the reason the request failed.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">
      Batch ID.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reference" type="string">
      Batch reference.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string | null">
      The `name` you sent.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">
      Currency code.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">
      See [Batch Statuses](#batch-statuses).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fee_source" type="string">
      `balance` or `amount`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_payouts" type="number">
      Payouts received.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="valid_payouts" type="number">
      Payouts that passed the checks. Only these are sent.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="invalid_payouts" type="number">
      Payouts left out, each with an `error`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_amount" type="number">
      Sum of the valid payouts' amounts.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_fee" type="number">
      Sum of their fees.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_vat" type="number">
      VAT on those fees.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_stamp_duty" type="number">
      Stamp duty, where it applies.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_debit" type="number">
      What leaves your wallet when the batch is approved.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approve_by" type="string | null">
      While `awaiting_approval`: when the batch expires if the owner has not approved it.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="progress" type="object">
      Once sent: how many payouts are `completed`, `processing` and `failed`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string">
      When the batch was created.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="checked_at" type="string | null">
      When the checks finished.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approved_at" type="string | null">
      When the owner approved it.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="completed_at" type="string | null">
      When the last payout finished.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="cancelled_at" type="string | null">
      When it was cancelled.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payouts" type="array">
      Every payout in the batch, in the order you sent them.
      <Expandable title="properties">
        <ResponseField name="row" type="number">
          Position in your request, from 1.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="reference" type="string">
          Your reference.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="check_status" type="string">
          `pending` (not checked yet), `valid`, `invalid` (see `error`), then `sending`, `sent`, or `failed` if the debit was refused.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="error" type="string | null">
          Why the payout was left out or failed to send.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="amount" type="number | null">
          Amount.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="fee" type="number | null">
          Fee.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="vat_amount" type="number | null">
          VAT on the fee.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="stamp_duty" type="number | null">
          Stamp duty.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="net_amount" type="number | null">
          What the recipient receives.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="total_debit" type="number | null">
          What this payout takes from your wallet.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="bank_code" type="string | null">
          Recipient bank code.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="bank_name" type="string | null">
          Recipient bank.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="account_number" type="string | null">
          Recipient account number.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="account_name" type="string | null">
          The account name the bank returned.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="narration" type="string | null">
          Your narration.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="payout_reference" type="string | null">
          Once sent: the payout's Rolla Pay reference, for [Verify Payout](/api-reference/verify-payout).
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="payout_status" type="string | null">
          Once sent: `pending`, `processing`, `completed` or `failed`.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="failure_reason" type="string | null">
          Why the payout failed.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
