## Overview

Initiate a payout to disburse funds from your Rolla Pay wallet. One endpoint serves both methods; the `destination` object decides which.

The two differ in more than their fields, so each has its own page:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bank Transfer Payout" icon="building-columns" href="/api-reference/bank-transfer-payout">
    **NGN.** Three steps — list banks, resolve the account, then pay. Requires a `name_enquiry_ref`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mobile Money Payout" icon="mobile" href="/api-reference/mobile-money-payout">
    **GHS** A single call. The phone number is the identity, so there is nothing to resolve.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

This page documents what both share.

## Common Parameters

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  The amount the recipient receives, in major units of the wallet's currency (e.g. `1000` means ₦1,000, not 1,000 kobo).

  By default the payout fee is charged on top of this, so your wallet is debited `amount + fee`. Use `fee_source: "amount"` to deduct the fee from the payout instead.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="currency" type="string" required>
  ISO 4217 currency code. Must match a wallet you have, and the method: `NGN` for bank transfers, `GHS` / `KES` / `ZMW` for mobile money.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="destination" type="object" required>
  The recipient. Its shape depends on `destination.type` — see [Bank Transfer](/api-reference/bank-transfer-payout) or [Mobile Money](/api-reference/mobile-money-payout).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="destination.type" type="string" required>
  Either `bank_transfer` or `mobile_money`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="narration" type="string">
  A description for the payout (max 200 characters).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="reference" type="string">
  Your unique idempotency key (max 50 characters). If provided, a retry with the same reference returns the original payout instead of creating a second one.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="fee_source" type="string" default="balance">
  Who absorbs the payout fee.

  - `balance` (default) — the fee is charged on top. Your wallet is debited `amount + fee`, the recipient receives exactly `amount`.
  - `amount` — the fee comes out of the payout. Your wallet is debited `amount`, the recipient receives `amount - fee`.

  With `amount`, any payout at or below the fee is rejected, because the recipient would receive nothing.

  <Warning>
    The default changed from `amount` to `balance`. Integrations that relied on the fee being deducted from the payout must now pass `fee_source: "amount"` explicitly — otherwise the same request debits `amount + fee` from your wallet and the recipient receives more than before.
  </Warning>
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">
  Indicates if the request was successful.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="message" type="string">
  Response message.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="reference" type="string">
      Rolla Pay payout reference (e.g. `PAYOUT-RP-1785413925129-40HC3YFOIE`).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="merchant_reference" type="string">
      Your custom reference, if provided.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="number">
      What leaves your wallet. Under `fee_source: "balance"` this is the requested amount plus the fee.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fee" type="number">
      The payout fee charged.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="net_amount" type="number">
      What the recipient receives.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">
      Currency code.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">
      Payout status: `pending`, `processing`, `completed`, `failed`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fee_source" type="string">
      Which fee mode was applied.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string">
      ISO 8601 timestamp.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Next Steps

After initiating a payout:

1. The payout status starts as `pending` and moves to `processing` once sent to the processor.
2. You will receive a [webhook](/api-reference/webhooks) notification (`payout.completed` or `payout.failed`) when the payout is finalized.
3. You can also poll the [Verify Payout](/api-reference/verify-payout) endpoint to check the status.
4. If a payout fails, funds are automatically credited back to your wallet.
