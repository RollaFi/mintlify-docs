---
title: 'Test Bank Transfers'
description: 'Test NGN bank transfer payments and virtual account credits with a test API key'
---

When you use a test secret key (`sk_test_*`), NGN bank transfer payments are simulated. You get a fake account number and the same webhooks as live, but no real account is opened and no money moves.

## How a test payment works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the payment">
    Call [Initiate Payment](/api-reference/initiate-payment) with `channel: "bank_transfer"`, as you would in live. You get back an account number to show your customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer pays">
    You don't need to do anything here. About 30 seconds later, a simulated customer transfers money into the account. The amount you charged decides how much they send (see [Choose the outcome](#choose-the-outcome)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You are notified">
    The payment becomes `successful` or `failed`, and you receive the webhook, exactly as in live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you'd rather control when the money arrives and how much it is, send the transfer yourself instead. See [Send a transfer yourself](#send-a-transfer-yourself).

## Example

```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.rollapay.com/transaction/initiate" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_your_test_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "reference": "test-transfer-001",
    "amount": 5000,
    "currency": "NGN",
    "channel": "bank_transfer",
    "customer": {
      "email": "customer@example.com",
      "name": "Test Customer"
    }
  }'
```

The response gives you the account to show your customer. The status is `pending` because the customer hasn't paid yet:

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "message": "Virtual account created successfully",
  "data": {
    "reference": "test-transfer-001",
    "status": "pending",
    "account_number": "5612345678",
    "bank_name": "NET Microfinance Bank",
    "account_name": "Your Business - Test Customer",
    "account_type": "dynamic",
    "amount": 5000,
    "expires_at": "2026-11-19T15:00:00.000Z"
  }
}
```

About 30 seconds later the payment becomes `successful`. Read the final status from the webhook or [Verify Transaction](/api-reference/verify-transaction), never from this response.

## Choose the outcome

The last two digits after the decimal point of `amount` decide what the simulated customer does. Every other amount is paid in full.

| `amount` | The customer sends | Final status | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| `5000` (or any amount not listed below) | ₦5,000, exactly what is owed | `successful` | About 30 seconds |
| `5000.01` | Half of what is owed | `failed` | About 30 seconds |
| `5000.02` | ₦1,000 more than what is owed | `successful`, for the amount owed | About 30 seconds |
| `5000.03` | Nothing | `failed` | When the account expires, 30 minutes later |

A payment that is short fails with the reason `Insufficient amount: expected 5000.01, received 2500.01`, the same message you get in live.

These amounts only trigger an outcome with a test key. With a live key, `5000.01` is just a normal payment of ₦5,000.01.

## Send a transfer yourself

Use this when you want to choose the amount and the moment the money arrives, for example to test a short payment of a specific amount.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a payment that waits for you">
    Create the payment with an `amount` ending in `.03`, for example `5000.03`. The simulated customer then never pays, so the account waits for your transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the transfer">
    Call `POST /sandbox/bank-transfer` with the `account_number` from step 1 and the amount the customer sends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you skip step 1 and use a normal amount, the simulated customer may pay before you do. Your transfer is then refused with `409`, because the payment is already complete.

```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.rollapay.com/sandbox/bank-transfer" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_your_test_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "account_number": "5612345678",
    "amount": 5000.03
  }'
```

<ParamField header="Authorization" type="string" required>
  `Bearer sk_test_...`. Live keys are refused.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="account_number" type="string" required>
  The 10-digit test account to pay into.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  How much the customer sends, in naira, with up to two decimal places.
</ParamField>

The transfer is settled straight away, so the response already shows the result:

```json 200
{
  "success": true,
  "message": "Simulated transfer sent",
  "data": {
    "account_number": "5612345678",
    "account_type": "dynamic",
    "amount": 5000.03,
    "reference": "test-transfer-001",
    "status": "successful"
  }
}
```

Sending at least the amount owed makes the payment `successful`. Sending less makes it `failed`. Either way, your webhook fires.

### Static virtual accounts

The same endpoint pays into a [static virtual account](/api-reference/virtual-accounts) you opened with a test key. Static accounts don't auto-pay, so this is the only way to test a credit to one.

Each transfer creates a new `successful` payment for the amount sent, as a real transfer would. The response has `account_type: "static"`, and `reference` is the new payment's reference.

### Errors

| Status | Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | | `account_number` or `amount` is missing |
| `400` | `INVALID_ACCOUNT_NUMBER` | `account_number` is not 10 digits |
| `400` | `INVALID_AMOUNT` | `amount` is not positive, or has more than two decimal places |
| `403` | | You used a live key |
| `404` | `ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` | No test account with that number belongs to your business |
| `409` | `PAYMENT_NOT_AWAITING_TRANSFER` | The payment is already `successful` or `failed` |
| `409` | `ACCOUNT_EXPIRED` | The account expired before the transfer |
| `409` | `ACCOUNT_DEACTIVATED` | The static account is deactivated |

## Webhooks

`payment.success` and `payment.failed` fire for test payments. They go to your **test** webhook URL and are signed with your **test** webhook secret. If you sent a `callbackUrl`, it is notified too.

## What's different from live

- **No real money.** Test account numbers can't receive money from a real bank. Payment accounts start with `56`, static accounts with `55`.
- **No settlement.** Test payments are never credited to a wallet, including your sandbox wallet. To fund a sandbox wallet, see [Test Payouts](/api-reference/test-payouts#funding-your-sandbox-wallet).
- **Fixed sender.** The payer is always `Sandbox Customer` at `Sandbox Bank`.
- **No processor minimums.** Your own settings still apply: bank transfer must not be switched off for NGN on your account, your amount limits apply, and fees are calculated as in live.
